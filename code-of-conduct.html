EIC Community

Code of conduct.

EIC is a trusted, curated community. By joining, you agree to the following:

  1. Keep conversations in the room

    We follow the Chatham House Rule. You can use what you learn, but don't identify who said it or attribute it to their firm without permission.

  2. Keep confidential information confidential

    Do not share confidential information, screenshots, private conversations, or sensitive information from within EIC.

    Leaking confidential information will result in immediate removal from the community.

  3. Don't share event links

    EIC event links are for approved community members only. Do not forward them to people outside the community.

    Know someone who should be there? Send them the EIC application instead.

  4. Don't bring unregistered guests

    Everyone attending an EIC event must be registered and approved in advance.

    Sharing event links or bringing unregistered guests will result in a warning. Three warnings will result in removal from the community.

  5. Be respectful

    Treat other members professionally and respectfully. Harassment, discrimination, or inappropriate behaviour may result in removal.

  6. Don't spam the community

    Do not use EIC, its member list, or community channels for mass sales outreach, unsolicited marketing, or spam.

EIC reserves the right to remove members whose behaviour compromises the trust or integrity of the community.
