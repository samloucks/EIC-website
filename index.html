About EIC

Investing in Canada is a small world. It shouldn't be a closed one.

Breaking into investing in Canada still runs on who you happen to know. The result is a market that recycles the same networks and overlooks a lot of talent along the way.

EIC was founded to change that. We bring together emerging investors from across the country — different firms, different asset classes, different backgrounds — and give them a genuine peer group: people to compare notes with, learn from, and eventually do business with.

No membership fees. No gatekeeping for its own sake. Just a serious community of people early in their investing careers who'd rather build it together.

“The best career advice I got came from someone two years ahead of me, not twenty.”

— Why we started EIC
