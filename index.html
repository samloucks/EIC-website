About EIC

A community built by and for the next generation of Canadian investors.

What makes venture special is the people you get to meet — people with different backgrounds, perspectives and experiences, all coming together to share ideas, discover opportunities and learn from one another.

In a world where technology, markets and ideas are evolving faster than ever, we believe the next generation of investors will help shape what comes next.

Emerging Investors Canada brings together early-career professionals, from Analyst to Principal, working in venture capital across firms, sectors and cities to build genuine relationships, exchange perspectives and create opportunities for one another. Through events, shared experiences and an active community, EIC gives early-career investors the chance to grow alongside the peers who will become the next generation of leaders in Canadian venture.

Our vision is to enrich junior VC culture in Canada, so the next generation of investors can learn and grow together.

— Why EIC exists

Who it's for

Built for people early in a venture career.

Our goal is to bring together a strong community of early-career venture professionals across Canada.

EIC is for people from Analyst to Principal working across the venture ecosystem, including:

  • Investing
  • VC platform
  • Venture debt
  • Venture studio
  • Growth buyout
  • Accelerator
  • Venture scout
  • VC adjacent roles

We create opportunities to meet peers, exchange ideas, share opportunities and build genuine relationships through events, gatherings and an active community.
