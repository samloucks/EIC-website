About EIC

A community built by and for junior VCs.

Emerging Investors Canada brings early-career VCs together from across firms and across the country. We do it the straightforward way: by hosting events and gatherings where meeting people is the point, and where real relationships have room to form.

What comes out of it is a genuine peer group — people at the same career stage to learn from, compare notes with, and stay in touch with as careers move.

Our vision is to enrich junior VC culture in Canada, so the next generation of investors can learn and grow together.

— Why EIC exists

Who it's for

Built for people early in a venture career.

Membership is designed for those in junior or emerging roles. If that sounds like you, we'd love to have you in the room.

  • Junior or emerging roles

    Associate level and below, or an equivalent career stage.

  • Early in your career

    Typically under 10 years of total professional experience.

  • Venture capital in Canada

    Investing across the Canadian venture ecosystem, at any fund or stage.
